A sealed medical supply crate with the Prometheus Initiative logo stenciled on the side.

The crate contains standard medical supplies: bandages, antiseptic, pain medication, and emergency trauma kits. Everything is neatly organized and labeled according to medical protocols.

One section of the crate appears to have been recently accessed - several vials of sedatives are missing, along with a portable medical scanner. There's a handwritten note tucked between the remaining supplies:

"Took scanner and sedatives to Medical Bay 2. Three more patients with the same symptoms. -Dr. Kim"

The medical scanner's absence is concerning. Whatever was happening in this facility, it was serious enough that medical personnel were scrambling to treat multiple cases simultaneously.
