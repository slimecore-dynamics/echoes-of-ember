DR. SARAH CHEN - RESEARCH JOURNAL
Project Prometheus - Artifact Analysis

March 10, 2087

We've made another breakthrough with the artifact. The energy readings continue to defy conventional physics. Dr. Martinez theorizes it might be some form of dimensional technology, but I'm not convinced. The patterns are too regular, too... intentional.

The artifact seems to respond to proximity. When researchers get close, the energy signature intensifies. We've had to implement strict protocols limiting exposure time to prevent... incidents.

March 12, 2087

Today's test confirmed my suspicions. The artifact is not just emitting energy - it's communicating. The patterns we've been recording aren't random fluctuations. They're a message. Or perhaps a warning.

I've recommended we pause testing until we can properly decode what we're seeing, but the board wants results. They don't understand the risk. This thing is far beyond anything we've encountered before.

March 14, 2087

Something is wrong. The artifact's activity has increased exponentially over the past 48 hours. No one has been near it, yet it continues to pulse with increasing intensity. The containment field is holding, but I don't know for how much longer.

I've noticed strange occurrences around the facility. Equipment malfunctions. Personnel reporting unusual dreams. And yesterday, three researchers called in sick with the same symptoms - severe headaches and temporal disorientation.

I'm submitting an emergency request to shut down the project. Whatever this artifact is, we're not ready to handle it.

[Final Entry]

March 15, 2087 - 13:47

The artifact is active. Containment failing. I can hear it now - not with my ears, but in my mind. It's calling to something. Or someone.

If anyone finds this journal, you need to understand: this was never just an artifact. It's a beacon. And now that we've activated it, there's no turning it off.

God help us all.
