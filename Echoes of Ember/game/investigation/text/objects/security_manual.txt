PROMETHEUS INITIATIVE
SECURITY PROTOCOLS MANUAL
Classification Level: 3

SECTION 1: ACCESS CONTROL

All personnel must display visible identification at all times while on facility premises. Access to restricted areas requires appropriate clearance level verification via biometric scanners.

Clearance Levels:
- Level 1: General Personnel (Common areas, cafeteria, dormitories)
- Level 2: Research Staff (Laboratories, data terminals)
- Level 3: Senior Research & Security (Artifact containment, classified archives)
- Level 4: Executive Command (All areas, emergency override authority)

SECTION 2: EMERGENCY PROCEDURES

In the event of a containment breach:
1. Sound facility-wide alarm
2. Initiate automatic lockdown protocols
3. Seal affected sectors
4. Evacuate non-essential personnel
5. Deploy containment teams to breach site

Emergency override codes are known only to Level 4 personnel and are changed weekly.

SECTION 3: ARTIFACT HANDLING

Special protocols apply to interaction with recovered artifacts:

- No direct physical contact without protective equipment
- Minimum safe distance: 5 meters for passive observation
- Active testing requires Level 3 authorization and full hazmat protocols
- All exposure must be logged and personnel monitored for 72 hours post-contact
- Any unusual symptoms must be reported to Medical immediately

FAILURE TO COMPLY WITH THESE PROTOCOLS MAY RESULT IN SEVERE DISCIPLINARY ACTION OR IMMEDIATE TERMINATION OF EMPLOYMENT.
